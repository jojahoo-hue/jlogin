## What Remains / Next Ideas

- **Python alias fix**: The `python` symlink points to 3.11 (no numpy), while packages install to 3.13. Consider updating the invocation in docs/README to use `python3.13`, or add a `python3.13 -m pip install` step in the setup instructions.
- **New scribe — attractor**: Strange-attractor plots (Lorenz, Clifford, etc.) appeared in earlier routines but are no longer listed in `PLOTS`. Could be worth restoring or adding.
- **Silhouette mode for plots**: Currently `--mode silhouette` is only documented for `sacred` commands. Extending it to `plot` commands would give cleaner Cricut-ready SVGs for harmonograph/spirograph outputs.
- **Colour variants**: Today's renders use default palettes. Exploring alternate colour maps (e.g. Congo tradition palette, warm earth tones) would complement Njaho's artistic direction.
- **Gallery pagination**: At 828 artefacts the static `index.html` may be slow. Adding client-side pagination or a "latest 50" default view could improve UX.
